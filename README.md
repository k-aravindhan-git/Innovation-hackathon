# Maruti Suzuki Innovation Hackathon
# Mobility for Bharat: Resilient Shared Energy Networks in NE India
This Pitch deck is used in the finals of the Maruti Suzuki Innovation Hackathon, which was evaluated by industry experts from IIM Calcutta and Maruti Suzuki India Limited.
> **Overview:** A strategic blueprint and pitch summary for deploying low-cost, flood-resilient, and hill-capable shared mobility solutions in Guwahati, Assam. This repository outlines the transition from rigid EV charging infrastructure to a modular, dual-vector swapping model (Li-ion + Hydrogen) designed for the global south.

## The Problem: The "Mobility Trilemma"
Metropolitan mobility models (designed for Delhi or Mumbai) fail in Northeastern India due to a unique set of constraints:

1. **Economic Sensitivity:** Driver-entrepreneurs ("Rickshawallahs") face "Energy Anxiety." Losing 4-5 hours to charge a battery translates to a 30-40% loss in daily wages.
2. **Ecological Volatility (Floods & Hills):** Guwahati is a topographical basin prone to severe flash floods. During monsoons, state-run transit operations (like ASTC's 256 electric buses) face major disruptions as flooded roads force service reductions. Furthermore, standard EVs lack the torque to navigate hill settlements like Nilachal.
3. **Infrastructure & Regulatory Gaps:** Grid power is frequently cut during floods for safety. On the regulatory front, the Regional Transport Authority (RTA) has prohibited slow-moving e-rickshaws on 56 major thoroughfares to ease congestion, restricting them strictly to 31 designated routes for last-mile connectivity. This necessitates a highly optimized, targeted fleet strategy.

## Solution Architecture: "Assam Shakti" Dual-Vector Mesh
A one-size-fits-all approach does not work. We propose a hybrid Hub-and-Spoke energy mesh, decoupling the battery/fuel from the vehicle.

### Vector A: Urban Core (Li-ion Swapping)
*   **Target:** Flat city basin, short commutes, restricted 31 designated last-mile routes.
*   **Tech:** L5 E-Rickshaws utilizing Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) BaaS.
*   **Resilience:** Swap stations built on "Machan" (Stilt) platforms elevated >4ft to survive floodwaters.
*   **Economics:** Running cost of **₹1.28/km**. Supported by the Assam EV Policy, which offers a 100% waiver on road tax and parking for 5 years, and a massive 90% waiver on electricity duty for EV charging stations.

### Vector B: Hills & Peri-Urban (Hydrogen Cartridges)
*   **Target:** Heavy agricultural logistics (vegetable corridors) and steep hill climbs (Kamakhya Temple).
*   **Tech:** Cargo 3-Wheelers using low-pressure Metal Hydride hydrogen cartridges. Real-world platforms like Biliti Electric's "FastMile" hydrogen 3-wheeler have already demonstrated a 130-mile range with a refueling time of under 3 minutes.
*   **Resilience:** Cartridge depots are passive (like LPG warehouses) and require zero electricity, ensuring transport continuity even when the city grid is down during floods.

## The Hydrogen Supply Chain Integration
To overcome the high upfront cost of Green Hydrogen, this project leverages local industrial assets in Assam:
* **Local Production:** NeuEN Green Energy is establishing a 10,000 metric tonnes per annum (MTPA) green hydrogen generation unit at the Numaligarh Refinery (NRL) on a build-own-operate basis. Slated to be operational by 2028, this will secure a massive, localized green hydrogen supply chain.
* **State Subsidies:** Assam's Clean Energy Policy offers a 100% electricity duty exemption for green hydrogen production and a 30% capital subsidy, driving the TCO (Total Cost of Ownership) towards the target of <₹1.00/km by 2030.

## Impact & Economics
* **Micro-Franchisee CAPEX:** ~₹11 Lakhs sets up a flood-proof micro-swap station, enabling local *kirana* store owners to become energy distributors.
* **Driver ROI:** Daily operational expenses drop from ₹370 (Petrol) to ₹165 (EV/Swap), generating **~₹6,000 in monthly savings** for low-income drivers.
* **Financial Inclusion:** Fintech integration allows for "Pay-As-You-Earn" micro-installments deducted digitally at the time of swapping, eliminating the need for traditional credit scores.

## Next Steps & Pilot
1. **Phase 1 (Immediate):** Deploy 500 Li-ion E-rickshaws on the 31 RTA-approved routes with elevated swap stations.
2. **Phase 2 (2026-2027):** Launch a 50-vehicle Hydrogen Cargo pilot on the North Guwahati vegetable corridor in anticipation of NRL's green hydrogen output.
3. **Phase 3 (2028+):** Scale passive hydrogen depots across upper Assam districts.
